Generic and specific facets of vulnerability for analysing
trade‐offs and synergies in natural resource management
==========================================================

This survey was run in Spring 2014. In involved interviews with union leaders ('leaders' sheet) and fishers ('fishers' sheet)

Lead researcher: Stefan Gelcich
Other researchers: J. Cinner, S. Tapia-Lewin









Columns
-------

--fishers sheet

* UnionID: union identifier
* ImportanceFishery: importance of fisheries for household livelihood (0=not important; 1=somewhat important; 2=important; 3=very important)
* ImportanceShoreCollection: importance of shore collection for household livelihood (0=not important; 1=somewhat important; 2=important; 3=very important)
* ImportanceMiddleman: importance of middleman activity for household livelihood (0=not important; 1=somewhat important; 2=important; 3=very important)
* ImportanceAgriculture: importance of agriculture for household livelihood (0=not important; 1=somewhat important; 2=important; 3=very important)
* ImportanceAgrSale: importance of agriculture for household income (0=not important; 1=somewhat important; 2=important; 3=very important)
* ImportancePaidJob: importance of salaried employment for household livelihood (0=not important; 1=somewhat important; 2=important; 3=very important)
* ImportanceTourims: importance of tourism for household livelihood (0=not important; 1=somewhat important; 2=important; 3=very important)
* ImportanceInformalJobs: importance of information jobs for household livelihood (0=not important; 1=somewhat important; 2=important; 3=very important)
* ImportanceOthers: importance of other activities for household livelihood (0=not important; 1=somewhat important; 2=important; 3=very important)
* OcMultiplicity(personsjob): total number of activities undertaken at the household level
* DifferentOcuppations: number of different activities undertaken at the household level
* NEmploymentChanges: did you change activity other the last 5 years? (yes=1; no=0)
* ParticipDecisionSyndicate: If a decision needs to be taken in the union, do you feel involved and how? (no=-1; passively involved=0; actively involved=1)
* ParticipDecisionTURF: Are you involved in making decisions regarding the management and / or use of marine resources? (no=-1; passively involved=0; actively involved=1)
* CommunityEvents: How often you participated in any event of the community and union, outside those of your family, in the last 12 months?
* CommunityOrganizations: In addition to the Union, how many other community organization do you belong to?
* Generator: Do you have a generator in your household? (yes=1; no=0)
* Electricity: Do you have electricity in your household? (yes=1; no=0)
* TV: Do you have a TV in your household? (yes=1; no=0)
* Flatscreen_tv: Do you have a flatscreen in your household? (yes=1; no=0)
* TV_Cable: Do you have cable TV in your household? (yes=1; no=0)
* Satellite_tv: Do you have satellite TV in your household? (yes=1; no=0)
* DVD: Do you have a DVD in your household? (yes=1; no=0)
* Fridge: Do you have a fridge in your household? (yes=1; no=0)
* Hot_water: Do you have hot water in your household? (yes=1; no=0)
* Interior_toilet: Do you have toilets inside your household? (yes=1; no=0)
* Plumbing: Do you have plumbing in your household? (yes=1; no=0)
* mobile_phone: Do you own a mobile phone? (yes=1; no=0)
* Bicycle: Do you own a bicycle? (yes=1; no=0)
* Motorbike: Do you own a motorbike? (yes=1; no=0)
* Car: Do you own a car? (yes=1; no=0)
* Alternate_transport: Do you own another transport? (yes=1; no=0)
* Cook_with_wood: Do you cook with hard fuel (wood) in your household? (yes=1; no=0)
* Charcoal: Do you cook with hard fuel (charcoal) in your household? (yes=1; no=0)
* Kerosene: Do you cook with diesel fuel in your household? (yes=1; no=0)
* Gas_electric: Do you cook with GPL in your household? (yes=1; no=0)
* RoofGrade: Rate the quality of your roof (very poor=0; excellent=7)
* floorGrade: Rate the quality of your floor (very poor=0; excellent=7)
* wallsGrade: Rate the quality of your walls (very poor=0; excellent=7)
* Second_house: Do you own a secondary house? (yes=1; no=0)
* Other_property: Do you own an other property?  (yes=1; no=0)
* Education: years of formal education (1=school; 13=university)
* TrustCommunity: How much do you trust people in your community? (not at all=-2; average=0; very much=2)
* TrustPolice: How much do you trust the police? (not at all=-2; average=0; very much=2)
* TrustLocalGov: How much do you trust the local government? (not at all=-2; average=0; very much=2)
* TrustNGO: How much do you trust NGOs? (not at all=-2; average=0; very much=2)
* TrustFisheryOfficer: How much do you trust the fisheries officers? (not at all=-2; average=0; very much=2)
* TrustUnionLeaders: How much do you trust your union leader? (not at all=-2; average=0; very much=2)
* TrustMiddlemen: How much do you trust the middlemen? (not at all=-2; average=0; very much=2)
* GearDiversity: How many gear do you use as part of your fishing activity?
* CatchPercentSold: What proportion of you catch do you sell to markets? (0=none; 1=all)
* CatchSelfConsumption: What proportion of you catch do you keep for self consumption? (0=none; 1=all)
* ComplianceAccess: How much do you comply with the access rules in your caleta? (-2=never comply; -1=rarely comply; 0=comply sometimes; 1=comply often; 2=always comply)
* ComplianceSize: How much do you comply with the minimum size regulations in your caleta? (-2=never comply; -1=rarely comply; 0=comply sometimes; 1=comply often; 2=always comply)
* ComplianceTAC: How much do you comply with the quota in your caleta? (-2=never comply; -1=rarely comply; 0=comply sometimes; 1=comply often; 2=always comply)
* InvolvedEnforcement: How involved are you in the surveillance and monitoring of your TURF (poaching)? (1=poorly involved; 7=very much involved)
* InvolvedMonitoring: How involved are you in the ecological monitoring of your TURF? (1=poorly involved; 7=very much involved)
* SatisfactionSynd: In general, how satisfied are you with your union? (1=poorly satisfied; 7=very satisfied)
* SatisfactionBestTURF: In general, how satisfied are you with your best TURF? (1=poorly satisfied; 7=very satisfied)

9999 indicate no answer.







--leaders sheet
* UnionID: union identifier
* Distance_TURF: Distance between the nearest TURF and the caleta (km)
* IncomeTURFNow: Current economic situation of the best TURF (1=poor; 7=excellent)
* BoundaryConfussionBest: Did you notice any confusion with the boundaries of your best TRUF? (2=no; 1=yes)
* EnforceBest: Level of enforcement of the best TURF (1=none; 2=only in good weather conditions; 3=often; 4=constantly)
* ExternalPoachingBest: Level of poaching in the best TURF from outsiders (1=never; 2=very little; 3=sometimes; 4=regularly)
* InternalPoachingBest: Level of poaching in the best TURF from union members (1=never; 2=very little; 3=sometimes; 4=regularly)
* GraduatedSanctions: Do you increase sanctions if someone recurrently breaches the rules? (1=yes; 0=no)
* ConflictPresence: Is there any conflict among union members? (0=never; 1=moderate/verbal; 2=violent/destructive)
* ConflictFreq: Frequency of internal conflicts (0=less than once a year; 1=more than once a year)
* ConflictResol: Are there any conflict resolution mechanism in place? If yes, are there effective? (1=poorly effective; 5=very effective)
* InterSyndConflict: Is there any conflict with other unions? (0=never; 1=moderate/verbal; 2=violent/destructive) 
* InterSyndConfResol: Frequency of external conflicts (0=less than once a year; 1=more than once a year)
* ContactLocalAuth: How often do you contact/interact with the local authorities? (days/year)
* ContactRegionalAuth: How often do you contact/interact with the regional authorities? (days/year)
* ContactFishService: How often do you contact/interact with the fisheries services? (days/year)
* ContactFunders: How often do you contact/interact with funders (FFPA, SERCOTEC, etc.)? (days/year)
* ContactNGOs: How often do you contact/interact with NGOs? (days/year)
* ContactConsultants: How often do you contact/interact with consultants? (days/year)
* ContactScientists: How often do you contact/interact with scientists? (days/year)
* ContactBanks: How often do you contact/interact with banks? (days/year)
* ContactFederation: How often do you contact/interact with fishers federations? (days/year)
* ContactOtherSynd: How often do you contact/interact with other syndicates? (days/year)
* ContactMiddlemen: How often do you contact/interact with middlemen? (days/year)
* ContactCompanies: How often do you contact/interact with private companies? (days/year)
* HospitalDistance: Distance between the union and nearest hospital (km)
* ConsRoomDist: Distance between the union and nearest dispensary (km)
* PrimarySchoolDist: Distance between the union and nearest primary school (km)
* MidHighSchoolDist: Distance between the union and nearest mid/highschool (km)
* MobileReceptionDist: Distance between the union and nearest phone reception (km)
* PoliceDist: Distance between the union and nearest police (km)
* BankDist: Distance between the union and nearest bank (km)
* PavedRoadDist: Distance between the union and nearest paved road (km)
* GasDist: Distance between the union and nearest gas station (km)
* RestaurantDist: Distance between the union and nearest restaurant (km)
* FreezerDist: Distance between the union and nearest freezer (km)
* IceMakerDist: Distance between the union and nearest ice maker (km)
* MaterialBoxesDist: Distance between the union and nearest boxes for fishing gear (km)
* DockDist: Distance between the union and nearest dock/boat ramp (km)
* FisheriesServiceDist: Distance between the union and nearest national fisheries service (km)
* NavalOfficialDist: Distance between the union and nearest captaincy (km)
* LocoProcessingPlantDist: Distance between the union and nearest loco processing plant (km)
