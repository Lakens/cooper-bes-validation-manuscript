Rossi et al., Functional Ecology, "Begging blue tit nestlings discriminate between the odour of familiar and unfamiliar conspecifics".

Bielefeld, 4 April 2017

Description of data file.

(2) File name: Rossi_FunctEcol_NestlingMassSurvival.csv

This data file was used to analyse the potential effect of the olfactory discrimination test trials on nestling mass and survival as observed on day 15 after hatching (day 0 = day of hatching of the first nestling of the brood).

Column labels:
- CaseNr -> Arbitrary case number.
- Nestbox -> Nestbox.
- HatchDate -> Date of hatching of the first nestling of the brood.
- AprilHDate ->	Hatch date in days from 1 April.
- NailCode -> Code indicating the combination of nail clips given to nestlings to identify them within their brood. Nail clips are applied at 4 days of age (day 0 = day of hatching of the first nestling of the brood) to mark them before ringing them from day 7 onward.
- Ring -> Ring number. Rings are applied from 7 days of age onward.
- BSManip -> Brood size manipulation category; "0_U": unmanipulated, "1_R": reduced, "2_C": control, "3_E": enlarged.
- OlfactoryTestBrood ->	"1": brood of which 2 nestlings were included in the olfactory discrimination experiment, "0": brood of which NO nestlings were included in the olfactory discrimination experiment.
- OlfactoryTestNestling -> "1": nestling was included in the olfactory discrimination experiment, "0": nestling was NOT included in the olfactory discrimination experiment.
- Mass_d4 -> Nestling mass at 4 days of age.
- Mass_d15 -> Nestling mass at 15 days of age.
- Alive_d15 -> "1": Nestling alive at 15 days of age, "0": Nestling is dead on day 15.
