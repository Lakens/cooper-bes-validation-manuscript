This file contains environmental, vegetation, litter decomposition and litter quality data from a litter decomposition experiments. 


Independent or predictive variables are listed from COLUMNS. 
Dependent or Response variables are from three types: environmetal (VEGETATION COVER AND RICHNESS, K VALUES, SOIL TEMP AND HUM, SOIL RESPIRATION)
litter decomposition (%ORGANIC MATTER REMAINING AND N remaining), AND Litter Quality
Measurements are in different units (described below). 
Missing/absent data are 'NA'.
