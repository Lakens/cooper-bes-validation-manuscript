METHODOLOGICAL INFORMATION

The study is based on location data obtained 2017-2022 from 17 common cranes and 55 greylag geese with GPS-equipped neckbands/legbands, made by Ornitela.

All captures and tagging of birds adhered to ethical requirements for research on wild animals after approval from the Animal Ethics Committee of central Sweden (common cranes: C104/10, C53/13, greylag geese: 5.8.18–03584/2017).
