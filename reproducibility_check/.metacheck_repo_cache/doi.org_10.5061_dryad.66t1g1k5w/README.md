# Bioacoustic dataset assessing the impact of festival music on bat activity

Species count and activity data derived from experimental bioacoustic study.

## Description of the data and file structure

Data is structured per species/species group for each music treatment condition, broken down by site.

Data counts are broken down into general bat activity (Bat.count) and feeding activity (FB.count) with the Buzz Ratio calculated as the ratio of bat calls to feeding calls, details of this can be found within the manuscript.

Measurements for appropriate environmental variables are included within the dataset with details of how this data was collected and used, as well as the statistical analysis undertaken can be found within the manuscript.

Indexs of bat activity and feeding activity are specific to field of bat ecology studies and further information regarding their calculation can be found within the materials and methods sections on manuscript. Environmental variable are measured using common methods (e.g. Temperature in celsius, Relative Humidity as a percentage, wind in miles per hour and Rainfall in mm) and are also described further within the manuscript.

## Sharing/Access information

Dataset is comprised of original raw data collected over the course of the experimental study and is not available in any other public domain. Data can be obtained through direct correspondance with authors.
